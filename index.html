About us

Built by students who got tired of asking, “Where did it all go?”

We are PCP-IS Solutions, a student team from the Institute of Computing at Davao del Norte State College. Our motto is simple: creating solutions for IS-related problems.

Finuity started with a problem we all knew. Allowance is gone by Thursday, the small buys (lunch, fare, load) add up, and utang gets forgotten. Nobody teaches us to plan money, and most budgeting apps feel made for people who already have the habit.

So we built the coach we wished we had. Finuity is not a bank or an e-wallet. You record your own money, which is exactly what makes you notice it, and short lessons teach you financial management along the way. It began as our entry concept for the Philippine Startup Challenge XI and is now a working app.

Mission

To help Filipino students understand their money by making it simple to track, plan and learn, one small daily habit at a time.

Vision

A generation of Filipino students who graduate with calm, confident money habits, and who know what is truly theirs to spend.

  1. Bills first. Show the number that matters: what is left after the things you must pay.
  2. Habits over lectures. Three-minute lessons and a daily streak beat a ten-page guide nobody reads.
  3. Your money, your call. You enter your own data, and you can hide the numbers whenever someone is looking.

Products & services

One app. Six ways it helps you keep more of your money.

Finuity runs in your phone’s browser and installs like an app. Here is what you get. The best part, seeing your own numbers, happens inside.

  1. 01

    Bills-first budgeting

    Add your bills and income and Finuity shows what is truly yours to spend this month, after bills. No more guessing from your wallet balance.

    Monthly budget limitBill reminders
  2. 02

    Log in seconds

    One-tap repeat for the things you buy every day, like lunch, fare and water. Snap a receipt to scan it. Three scans are free.

    One-tap repeatReceipt scanSpending by category
  3. 03

    Wallets and utang, in one place

    Track cash, e-wallets and bank balances by hand, and keep a clear list of who owes you and who you owe, with due dates and a ready-to-send reminder.

    AccountsUtang trackerHide numbers
  4. 04

    Savings goals that you can see

    Name a goal, set a target and watch it fill. A new laptop, an emergency fund, a trip home. Small ipon adds up faster when you can see it.

    GoalsProgress tracking
  5. 05

    Fin, your coin coach

    Fin is a pixel coin with a graduation cap who cheers your wins, nudges you before you overspend and levels up as your habit grows. Keep your streak alive, and use a freeze when life gets busy.

    Streaks & freezesLevelsWeekly recap
  6. 06

    Learn, in short lessons

    Take a three-minute quiz and unlock lessons built around how you actually spend: budgeting, saving first, smart spending and money safety.
